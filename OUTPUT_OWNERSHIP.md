# Your music stays yours

SlimeDragon Audio and the SlimeyBeats-EZ-Master contributors claim **0% ownership and 0% royalties** in audio you create, edit, master, or export with this software.

You keep all rights you hold in your recordings, compositions, performances, mixes, and resulting masters. You may release, sell, stream, license, or otherwise use your outputs without paying the project or crediting the app. We do not require a watermark, audio tag, revenue share, publishing share, or exclusive license to your work.

## Software credit is separate

The MIT license applies to the project's original software, documentation, and original bundled artwork. Keep its copyright and license notice when copying or redistributing the software or substantial portions of its source. You do **not** need to put that notice in your music or give the app a production credit.

Third-party dependencies and separately credited assets keep their own licenses. Using the app does not change the rights in somebody else's samples, recordings, compositions, or other material. The project grants no rights it does not hold and does not decide whether an output qualifies for copyright protection.

The bundled example recording is separately dedicated to the public domain under CC0 as documented in [the example credits](examples/audio/CREDITS.md). It does not create an attribution requirement for your outputs.
