This is an existing released electronic recording, not an untouched studio premaster. The demo uses a 45-second excerpt starting at 16 seconds, decoded to WAV24 with short fades and a linear headroom adjustment to approximately −20 LUFS. SlimeyBeats then processes it with Neutral/Gentler defaults into `demo-master.wav`. The two MP3 listening previews are linearly matched to approximately −20 LUFS.
