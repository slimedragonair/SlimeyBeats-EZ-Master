# ImageGen slime-frame provenance

Created November 12, 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool, with `transparent_background: true` for both calls. No CLI fallback, API key or Python image editing was used. The accepted second PNG was copied unchanged into `slimeybeats-slime-frame.png` (1586 × 992 RGBA). The first, thicker generation was not shipped.

Input roles: the 0.3.2 native Manual EQ capture was the border/layout edit target; `Ancient SlimeDragon reference sheet.png` and the accepted `slimeybeats-dragon-portrait.png` were material/style references. The original references were not modified. The second call edited only the first generated frame.

The native app uses UV slices and uniform scale for droplets; its own editable text, controls and plots are never part of the generated image. Source-specific crop coordinates and alpha observations are recorded in ASSETS.md and tested in the border module. A replacement PNG needs a new crop/layout review.

## First-generation prompt (material exploration)

Use case: precise-object-edit / production UI artwork. Input 1 is the existing SlimeyBeats desktop screenshot: replace its thin procedural border with a MUCH more refined illustrated slime frame, then deliver ONLY that isolated frame asset on a genuinely transparent canvas; remove every UI element, all text, buttons, graphs, panels, logos and the portrait from the output. Inputs 2 and 3 are STYLE/MATERIAL REFERENCES ONLY, the user's original SlimeDragon and its accepted portrait; do not alter those reference files. Match their translucent dark teal slime, glassy aqua highlights, subtle lavender edge reflections, deep blue-green internal depth and tiny restrained amethyst undertones. The current thin straight tubing and repetitive little rain drops are specifically rejected. Create sculpted, organic, viscous SLIME, with irregular pooling shoulders, surface-tension membranes, varied narrow necks, and weighted rounded hanging bulbs. ALL drips obey gravity and hang vertically DOWNWARD from connected slime above them. Unequal natural lengths and asymmetric spacing; no upward-facing drops, no sideways arrows, no detached floating droplets, no uniform icicles or identical teardrops. Premium polished fantasy illustration matching the dragon, carefully shaded wet volume rather than neon line art or flat green cartoon goo. Canvas: wide landscape approximately 16:10, straight front view rectangular desktop perimeter, NO perspective. Most of the canvas including the large central opening is truly transparent. Keep slime restrained along left/right/bottom edges (about 1.2% of canvas width); corner pools can be slightly broader, but keep generous clear center. The upper rim has only a thin layer over the left third reserved for branding and over the rightmost sixth reserved for Analyze. Put 5 to 7 beautifully shaped, visibly weighted drips in the otherwise empty TOP-CENTER span from roughly 34% to 82% of canvas width, longest only about 5% of canvas height. Side goo should subtly sag down as rivulets inside its narrow edge band; bottom goo settles in shallow pooled lobes, never hangs upward. No dragon heads, extra characters, skeleton/bone frame, text, gems sticking into the center, grids, drop shadows or glow haze across transparency. Keep every important tip inside the canvas; no clipped drips. Actual usable RGBA game/UI border artwork, not an app mockup, not a screenshot, not a checkerboard-painted background.

## Second-generation prompt (accepted source)

Edit ONLY the supplied transparent slime-frame asset into a thinner practical production UI frame. Preserve its excellent illustrated SlimeDragon material, dark translucent blue-green gel, aqua wet highlights, lavender rim reflections, organic membranes, genuine transparent center, downward gravity, landscape rectangular outline and empty central opening. The first version is too bulky: narrow ALL perimeter bands to roughly ONE THIRD their current thickness. At this approximately 1600x1000 source resolution, left/right bands should normally be only 12–22 pixels wide, the upper and lower rims only 8–14 pixels deep, and corner pools at most 28 pixels into the opening. Remove oversized corner masses; no large melted planks. Preserve beautifully shaded volume even at this smaller thickness, not a flat neon outline. Shorten the TOP CENTER hanging drips to 35–65 pixels total from the canvas top, not the current 170 pixels. Give each a believable pooled shoulder, gently tapering neck and a rounded weighted terminal bulb about 9–17 pixels across; scale those bulb shapes proportionally, do not squash them. Vary lengths, neck widths and spacing organically. Keep all longer top drips between 35% and 80% canvas width; outside that span the thin top rim must leave logo/title and Analyze areas clear. Side rivulets sag down only inside their 12–22-pixel side band. The bottom is a shallow settled pool, never inverted drops. Keep slime visually attached to the outer edge, safe transparent padding of just 2 pixels to prevent clipping. Eliminate detached cyan/purple fringe specks and outer glow haze, while preserving attached reflective edges. Output ONLY the isolated RGBA frame on real transparency, no background or checkerboard, no labels, UI, dragon heads, extra objects, gemstones, perspective, borders within the center, or additional ornament. This is the same artwork precisely adapted to narrow desktop edge gutters, with natural droops and clean anti-aliased alpha.

The generator did not meet every requested pixel limit literally. The app therefore fits the crown into the measured header at uniform scale, tiles side ribbons without flipping, uses fixed corner patches and never paints the central opening. Native captures, rather than the prompt alone, establish the delivered fit.
