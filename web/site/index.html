A little guidance

Local recovery

Off by default

Opt in to store audio, EQ, settings and editable Studio imports in this browser’s IndexedDB on this device. No uploads. Someone with access to this browser profile may be able to access saved audio. Clearing site data, private browsing, or browser storage cleanup can remove it.

Storage limits and recovery details

Up to three completed points per tab session, within 128 MiB per project and 256 MiB / 12 points total. These are recovery budgets, not editing limits. A crash can lose edits since the last completed save.

Pending file-picker/ZIP selections and transport position are not recovered. Studio rebuilds up to 128 committed edits and retains its normal bounded undo history, uncommitted stem faders and tool controls.

Delete all recovery points for this site and disable autosaves in all tabs? Current tab audio and downloaded files stay intact.

Restore starts stopped and requires an empty workspace. Open a new tab to preserve current work. Each tab saves its own session. Where browser locks are available, active sessions must be closed before restoration; no lock is stolen. Without locks, restoration copies a point into this tab and leaves its source untouched.

    Report a problem

    Create a small text report, review it, then choose whether to copy or download it. No audio, raw logs, filenames, song names, custom profiles or paths are collected as technical context. Nothing is sent automatically.

    Some obvious paths, links, email addresses and IP addresses are filtered from your description. Other personal details can remain. Review every line before sharing.
